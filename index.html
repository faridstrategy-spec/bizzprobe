The Problem

You're not blind. You're just not looking where it hurts.

Every business has a story it tells itself — in board decks, dashboards, and quarterly reviews. Most of that story is true. Some of it isn't. And the parts that aren't true are rarely the parts anyone is watching.

Revenue can decline while nobody agrees why. Teams can leave while HR reports "stable culture." Systems can look modern while quietly running on borrowed time. BizzProbe exists to close that gap — between what leadership believes, and what is actually happening.

Unexamined risk compounds silently.
